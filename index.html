About

Engineering profile

I am an Electronics and Communication Engineering student at KVG College of Engineering, graduating in 2027 and currently in the 7th semester. My interest sits where digital hardware meets the software that runs on it: how a processor fetches, decodes and executes an instruction, and how firmware talks to sensors and actuators through real interfaces.

On the VLSI side I work in SystemVerilog and Verilog, building modules and verifying them in simulation. On the embedded side I write firmware for ESP32 and Arduino boards and integrate sensors and motor drivers into complete systems.

What I built

Control logic for PondBot on an ESP32 in Embedded C: GPIO, PWM and interrupt-driven sensing. Ultrasonic, IMU and magnetometer sensors are interfaced over I2C and UART, and the motor driver and conveyor are controlled from the same firmware.

What I learned

Working with real sensors reinforced digital logic and timing fundamentals: bus protocols have to be timed correctly, interrupts must stay short, and sensing, decisions and actuation must be kept as separate stages.
